#4471 was merged by subject line (decision 0001): “the desk merges on subject alone; nobody has to read the body.” Reading the bodies and checking cause instead, not just subject, splits it into three bugs with three different fixes.

Filing one ticket per cause here, each with its count. #4471 stays open for the one cause that is actually its majority cause; the other two get their own tickets below.

The three tickets

  1. Parser flips month/day — the shared parser reads every date month-first, flipping it for every day-first-locale tenant on app/email and on booking-v2 forms. This is #4471’s real, majority cause — the row the table above marks as this ticket’s own.
  2. Wrong form hint on booking-v3 — a different bug, same-looking symptom: three tenants on booking-v3 see a MM/DD/YYYY hint despite being day-first locales. Channel-specific (form only), so it can’t be the shared parser. The table marks this cause wrong for #4471: “not this ticket — no ticket of its own,” until now.
  3. A one-day shift, not a swap — 5 reports that desk-notes.json’s oneDayMove list already set apart as read differently from their filed cause, but which never got split out. Not a digit-order bug at all: dates land exactly one day off, which a month/day swap mostly can’t produce.

Lesson

Subject-line dedupe is fast and that’s the right trade during an incident (triage says so, and it’s still right). But “known: one issue, one fix queued” was true of the subject line, not of the reports. A ticket this size is worth one pass through causes.ts before the fix ships — the signal was already sitting in data the desk exports, in a footnote (oneDayMove) it had already written and a channel column it already collects.
